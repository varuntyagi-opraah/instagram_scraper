## Still open: needs a decision or more data

1. **Your scrape script uses `--detail none`** for both passes. Pass 2 will also have no
   likes/comments. Options: add the Meta token before pass 2, or use `--detail signals` (about
   30 extra requests per creator without Meta). Those accounts will refresh automatically later
   either way.
2. **A disclosure attaches to every @mention in the caption.** "#ad shot by @photographer" makes
   the photographer a `collaboration_disclosed` counterpart. Most such handles land in "other
   collaborators" (unclassified), not "brands", but growing `config/brands.csv` is what makes the
   brand list clean. Look at `data/kb/brands.csv` and the top "other collaborators" every month.
3. **Renamed handles.** The old handle returns `not_found`. History stays under the old account
   id, but the new handle is not discovered automatically. Check `not_found` rows in
   `run_status.csv` after each run and fix the roster.
4. **46 handles sit under two vendor codes.** That is probably duplicate CRM records, not two
   creators. Both are kept and linked; clean up in the CRM (`roster_review.csv` lists them).
5. **Reels plays without Meta**: the logged-out Reels tab gives only the first 12 Reels, so plays
   cover about 5 of 21 Reels. Meta's `view_count` fixes this.
6. **Instagram layout or doc_id changes** would make pages parse as empty. The five-in-a-row
   pause and your watchdog catch it. The fix is upgrading the pinned Instaloader and
   re-running the tests against freshly saved pages.
7. **Undated Reels-tab entries** (no caption or date) are not in the window. They only add
   plays to posts we already know.
8. **`data/raw/` grows** with every full run (roughly 0.1–0.8 MB per creator). Prune folders older
   than about 90 days, or run with `--no-raw` once the parsers are trusted.
9. **YouTube Shorts are guessed by duration** (≤ 3 min). The API has no Shorts flag.
10. **Shares on Instagram**: requested from Meta (`shares_count`). If Business Discovery refuses
    it for other accounts, the scraper drops the field automatically and shares stay blank.
    Reliable shares, saves, reach and audience need creator-consented Instagram Login.
11. **Don't put `data/opraah.db` in Drive** (see docs/DRIVE.md). Sync the generated `data/kb/`.
12. **Unfinished runs stay open** (run 1 from the pilot). This is harmless; `status` shows the
    newest one.

## Compatibility notes

- `data/exports/creators.csv` keeps its columns, because `opraah_data/cleanup/clean_roster.py`
  reads `instagram_username`, `status`, `posts_in_sample`, `top_themes`, `brands_worked_with` and
  `last_collected_at`.
- The database upgrades itself in place (schema v2) the first time new code opens it. Your running
  pass 1 keeps working, and the export and pass 2 your script starts afterwards use the new code.
